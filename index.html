Technology

We write our own simulations for the worlds in the game.

  • Planets

    Each planet starts from moving tectonic plates. Where plates collide, the crust folds into mountain ranges. Rivers then cut valleys, slopes wear down, and we work out the climate and ocean currents on top.

  • Plants

    Each plant starts from a genome and grows step by step where it stands. Its growing tips add shoots, leaves and roots, paid for with the light and water it gets.

  • Clouds

    We're writing a large-eddy simulation: a model of moist air in which rising eddies mix and cool, and cloud forms wherever the air saturates. We check it against BOMEX, a published reference case for trade-wind cumulus clouds.
